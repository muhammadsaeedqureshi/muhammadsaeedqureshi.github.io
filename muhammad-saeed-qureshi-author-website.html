About the author

Muhammad Saeed Qureshi is an independent author and publisher from Pakistan. He creates practical, reader-friendly books - from large-print puzzle books for seniors and Quran memorization trackers to beautifully designed coloring books for children and inspiring home interior guides. He also writes fiction. His goal is simple: useful books, honestly made, that readers return to.
